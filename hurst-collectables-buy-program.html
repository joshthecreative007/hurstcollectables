How the price is set

Every offer is pulled from current sold comps for that exact cert number — not a guess, not a lowball flat rate. You can watch us look it up.

Comic comp value × 30–40%
= your offer
Minimum offer on any accepted slab: $50

Why a percentage, not a flat number. A flat buy price either overpays on common books or badly underpays on keys. Pricing off the comp keeps every offer fair to you and sustainable for us.

Comps pulled live. We check the certification number against current graded-market data before naming a number — same information you could look up yourself.
